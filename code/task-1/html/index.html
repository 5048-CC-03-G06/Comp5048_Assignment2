Air Quality Pattern Discovery

Find groups of hours with similar pollution profiles by brushing the linked views, save them, and follow how their membership and defining attributes change over time.

Parallel coordinates

Each line is one hour. Drag vertically on an axis to brush a range (brushes on several axes combine); click an axis to clear its brush. Drag an axis label sideways to reorder axes. Thick lines are the median profile of each saved group.

Pollutant relationships

Drag a box over points to select a candidate group.

Daily profile heatmap

Daily medians of the visible hours, darker = higher (same axis scale as the parallel coordinates). The left bar counts selected hours per day. Click a date to select that day.

Selection and saved groups: which hours are they?

For each hour of day, weekday and month: the share of visible hours in that slot that belong to the group. Medians are shown next to the median of all visible hours.

Task 2: Temporal analysis

Group membership by month and hour of day

Each cell is one hour of day in one month. Darker = a larger share of that slot's complete hours belongs to the group. Hatched = no complete hours; a dot = fewer than 5 hours. Read down a column to see an hour change group over the year.

What defines the group each month

Rows are months, columns are attributes. Red = the group's median is higher than the other hours in that month, blue = lower, grey = no difference. Numbers are the group medians. Read down a column to see an attribute gain or lose importance.

Monthly parallel coordinates

One small parallel-coordinates plot per month with the same axes. Coloured lines are saved groups (dark = current selection); thick lines are their medians for that month.
